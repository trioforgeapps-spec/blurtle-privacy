Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters while you play.

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Welcome to Blurtle

Blurtle is a word puzzle game developed and operated independently.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Blurtle processes, why it is needed, and how players can contact us about their data.

1. Information We Collect

Blurtle may collect or store the following:

  • Player information: A chosen username, avatar preferences, and an anonymous account identifier.
  • Gameplay data: Guesses, game results, statistics, scores, and daily streaks.
  • Multiplayer activity: Room codes, match participation, results, and rematch activity.
  • Game preferences: Sound, haptic, and reminder settings, which may be saved locally.
  • Support information: Details you voluntarily provide when contacting us.

Blurtle does not require players to provide their real names or email addresses to play.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used to operate the game, remember player progress, calculate statistics, support multiplayer matches, maintain game functionality, and respond to support requests.

3. Third-Party Services

Blurtle uses Supabase for backend services, including anonymous authentication, database storage, and multiplayer features.

Supabase may process technical information necessary to provide its services. Learn more through the Supabase Privacy Policy .

Blurtle does not currently integrate advertising networks or separate analytics services.

4. Daily Reminders

Players can choose whether to receive optional Daily Word reminders. Reminder preferences are stored on the device.

Notification permissions can be changed through the device settings.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell player information.

Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate Blurtle, such as Supabase. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Account and gameplay information may be retained while a player's anonymous account remains active.

Players can request permanent deletion directly in Blurtle by going to Settings → Delete Player Data → Delete Account Permanently and confirming the request. The feature becomes available when account deletion is enabled in the released app.

A successfully completed request deletes the player's anonymous authentication account and associated server-side Daily Word records, including saved guesses and results, as well as multiplayer rooms involving that player.

Removing a multiplayer room can also end a shared match for another player. Other players' accounts and unrelated Daily Word records are not deleted.

After the server confirms deletion, Blurtle attempts to clear locally saved player progress, including Play Now statistics, and signs out the deleted account. If local cleanup does not succeed, some information may remain on that device until it is cleared.

Limited deletion-processing records may be retained to verify the outcome and investigate problems. Support messages sent separately to us are not automatically deleted by the in-app process.

If deletion does not complete, or you want help with your information, contact trioforge.apps@gmail.com. We may need to verify the anonymous account involved.

7. Children's Privacy

Blurtle is designed as a general-audience word game. We do not intentionally request children's real names or personal contact information.

If you have concerns about a child's information, please contact us.

8. Security

We use third-party infrastructure to help store and process game information. We aim to protect player information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when Blurtle's features or data practices change. The latest version will appear here, together with its updated date.

10. Contact Us

For questions about privacy, support, or data deletion, please contact:
